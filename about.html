About Student Stay SA

Making student accommodation information easier to explore.

Who We Are

Student Stay SA is a proposed student accommodation information service designed to help university and college students explore accommodation near their campuses.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with clear and useful accommodation information so they can compare options and make informed decisions.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted starting point for students searching for convenient and affordable accommodation near campus.

Our Audience

We focus on university and college students, new students, returning students, and parents or guardians helping students find accommodation.
